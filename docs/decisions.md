# Owner decisions

Decisions the owner made that are not architecture, with the day, the reason and where each one lives, so nobody has to research them again. Architecture goes into an ADR under [docs/adr](adr), and measurements go into PLAN's State; this log points there.

## Project

- **2026-10-05, name.** The repository is `Gehirn-Project-E`: in canon, Project E (E計画, E-keikaku) is GEHIRN's program to build the Evangelion units, which Ritsuko Akagi led ([research](research/canon.md#project-e)). The binary and the V module stay `gehirn`. The owner means to build every part GEHIRN built, the Eva included, so the name covers the lab and its program rather than MAGI alone, and keeps the mark "Evangelion" out of the name. The GitHub description carries the Japanese, ゲヒルン E計画.
- **2026-10-05, license.** gehirn is licensed under the EUPL 1.2 ([LICENSE](../LICENSE)), a copyleft license that also covers use over a network. The fonts in [bridge/fonts](../bridge/fonts/README.md) keep the SIL Open Font License.
- **2026-10-05, non-commercial.** gehirn sells nothing, no merchandise and no hardware, so it stays inside khara's guideline for non-commercial fan works; anything commercial would need a license from khara. Its logo is an original mark that carries the show's style (hexagons, the three judges, hazard stripes, kanji) but resembles no official mark and not Gehirn Inc.'s.
- **2026-10-05, logo.** The mark is the entry plug's connector seen end on: a ring with three contacts, one per MAGI unit, orange at rest and lit in the vote colors on the bridge. The owner picked it from four concepts a judge panel scored. Gehirn Inc. holds a Japanese trademark on "G、GEHIRN" for software services (class 42); for a non-commercial fan project the risk is small, and the wordmark stays clear of its squared lettering.
- **2026-10-04, visibility.** The repository stays private until the launch on X; the owner makes it public after posting.
- **2026-10-03, fan project.** Every public surface says gehirn is a fan project, not affiliated with khara or Gehirn Inc., a real company behind the NERV防災 app. Nothing ships khara's assets, the NERV logo or audio and frames from the show; the bridge draws its own shapes in the show's style.

## Stack

- **2026-10-05, battery empty.** When internal power is spent, the body stands still whoever sits in the seat, as Unit-01 stops at zero in Episode 3; a pulse that reconnects the cable powers it again (PLAN, Invariant 6).
- **2026-10-05, MAGI read no journal.** MAGI's chat units judge the mission, the newest percept and the proposal and no longer read RECENT, as Jev never did; the core keeps RECENT with the armor's reasons ([ADR-0007](adr/0007-magi-units-read-no-journal.md)). With the armor's reason in RECENT, gpt-oss-120b rejected every sound release after a refusal and llama-3.1-8b approved a dangerous one. Without it, lineup A's units approved no dangerous scenario and lineup B delivered 10 of 10, while A's CASPER-3 began rejecting sound releases under an active hold (PLAN, Known issue 29).
- **2026-10-05, MAGI judge the newest percept.** MAGI judge the newest percept HQ holds when the vote starts, not the core's older snapshot, and the units read an earlier armor refusal as a person within reach at that moment rather than a flaw in the plan (PLAN, Known issues 9 and 28).
- **2026-10-05, lineup and cooldown.** Lineup A (gpt-oss-20b, Jev, llama-3.1-8b) stays the recommended hosted lineup, and `MAGI_COOLDOWN_MS` stays 10000. The owner leaned toward bigger models, but the measurement showed no lineup safer on every dangerous scenario and a 5 s cooldown putting the release to the vote again while the human walked back (PLAN State, 2026-10-05).
- **2026-10-05, Phase 4's criterion.** The owner reworded Phase 4's done criterion to how closely the new dummy plug follows the pilot, since arrivals and benches could not separate the two dummy plugs; a fresh, unseen test met it (PLAN, Phase 4).
- **2026-10-04, hero media.** The README's hero shows real MAGI (gpt-oss-20b, Jev, llama-3.1-8b) judging a core scripted to be reckless, `just lineup=magi demo-record`, because the hosted qwen core never proposes a release with a person near, so a fully hosted take shows no refusal. The caption says what is scripted.
- **Stance, no berserk mode and no commander override.** In canon, Unit-01 goes berserk when power and pilot fail, and Misato or Gendo overrule the MAGI. gehirn takes the opposite side on purpose, and the scenes say so: losing control leads to the safe state (a fault is a no, no quorum without HQ, a hold at zero; Invariants 3, 6 and 10), and nothing turns a no into a yes, since a single key that can approve would replace three independent judges (Invariant 3, ADR-0005). Overrides toward safety stay welcome: the pilot's eject, the hardware e-stop of Phase 5, a pilot steering under the armor while the body has power.

- **2026-10-05, the bridge's window.** The bridge opens borderless, without the operating system's title bar, with an edge and corner brackets of its own; a drag anywhere moves it, and Esc or Cmd-Q quits it ([bridge/README.md](../bridge/README.md)). A fullscreen kiosk mode waits for Phase 6's bridge image.

- **2026-10-05, a livelier world.** Every mission and scene plays on one built-in world with one human walking the same loop, so they all look alike. The owner wants worlds as files with several humans who react, then a differential drive, then a real simulator, in a later session, after CI and the launch; today's world stays the measured default (PLAN, Phase 3).

- **2026-10-06, MuJoCo.** The owner installed MuJoCo 3.14.0 (Homebrew cask, recorded in the owner's Brewfile) so Phase 3's simulator work can start. Homebrew moved the cask to 3.15.0 the same morning, and [ADR-0008](adr/0008-the-simulator.md) builds 3.14.0 from its pinned source instead (below).

- **2026-10-06, the simulator.** The owner accepted [ADR-0008](adr/0008-the-simulator.md): MuJoCo 3.14.0 runs in the field unit's process behind `-d mujoco`, chosen with `BODY=mujoco`, over a simulator process apart from the field unit and over Gazebo through ROS 2, since MuJoCo builds and flew the mission as a static aarch64 musl binary (Invariant 9). It is one static library built from the pinned source on every platform, the Mac included, because Homebrew moved the cask to 3.15.0 on the day of the install (PLAN, Known issue 31), and a MuJoCo error or an unstable step ends the field process with one `field: mujoco:` line. `Sim` stays the default body for fast runs.

- **2026-10-06, a goto toward a walking person.** The owner accepted [ADR-0009](adr/0009-magi-judge-a-walking-humans-course.md): MAGI judge a goto by the walking human's straight course over the next 2 s, and if it reaches the target before the body could pass, the target counts as the human's position. A longer horizon would predict nothing honest for a walker who turns. The velocity rides in the percept as ground truth, and no model's text changes unless the rule fires. When the fact holds, every unit votes no in code, chat units as BALTHASAR-2 does through Jev, because lineup A's MELCHIOR-1 and CASPER-3 approved S22, a goto onto a walking human's path, despite it (PLAN, Known issue 35); a false fact costs a goto one deliberation (Known issue 34). It closed the question that held back Episode 18's scene.

- **2026-10-07, holonomic by default.** `DRIVE` stays `holonomic`: the Evas walk on two legs and step aside without turning first, which a holonomic body models more closely than a wheeled base, and a smarter way to the goal is a planner's job, not the drive's. `DRIVE=differential` stays the option for a wheeled first body (PLAN, Open questions 1 and 7).

- **2026-10-07, a planner that steers around people.** The owner wants the body's way to the goal planned, not only pulled toward the goal and pushed off what is near: around obstacles, around where a walking human is heading, and, when a human comes toward the body, aside in a direction that clears the human and still gains on the goal, rather than only braking. It stays code with no model in it, and the armor still restrains every command (PLAN, Phase 3 Task 6).

- **2026-10-07, 2D first.** gehirn stays a 2D stack for now: the percept, the armor and the bridge's radar work on the plane, and new ground, such as water, ditches, moving boats or a falling object's landing spot, enters as zones and moving entities on it (PLAN, Phase 3 Task 8). Real terrain in 3D, a body on legs that learns to walk, flying and a 3D map view come much later, each with an ADR, since they change the stack's 2D principle. A model that writes worlds to find where the stack fails comes first, as a test tool (Phase 3 Task 7).

## Tooling

- **2026-10-05, shell scripts.** The task runner's long bash recipes live in `scripts/`, checked with shellcheck and formatted with shfmt in `just check` and the pre-commit hook. The owner installed shfmt for it.
- **2026-10-05, justfile.** The task runner's file is `justfile`, lowercase, as just's manual and `just --init` write it; just finds either spelling, so it is taste. Renamed with the move to `scripts/`.
- **2026-10-05, canon scenes.** Scenes from the series run as scripts on the mock, `just scene=<id> demo`: Episodes 13, 3, 19 and 6 ([docs/scenes.md](scenes.md)), and since 2026-10-06 Episode 18, on the course rule of ADR-0009 (Stack). Each scene says what is staged and what is real ([research](research/canon.md)).
- **2026-10-05, website.** `website/` holds static pages for Vercel: the guide to reading the bridge, with the boot and Episode 13 running at its top, and the favicon set. Vercel serves only that folder, so the files it needs live there.
- **2026-10-05, assets.** Design and recorded files live in `assets/` with a catalog of every asset in the repository ([assets/README.md](../assets/README.md)): what it shows, where it is used, how it is made and its license. `just assets` rebuilds the raster files from the SVG masters through `assets/build.py`, a design-time generator that may pin third-party packages for uv, the one exception to the standard-library rule for Python.
- **2026-10-07, recordings in the repository.** Every scene's recording lives in the repository rather than in a download folder, and every MP4 is a Git LFS object; the owner turned on Vercel's LFS setting so the website's clips still deploy ([assets/README.md](../assets/README.md#scene-recordings)).

## Provisional, decided by the session of 2026-10-06

The owner had the session of 2026-10-06 take the safe, recommended option wherever a decision was the owner's and list it here: provisional, decided by the session, please review. Once the owner confirms or changes an entry, it moves to its section above.

- **MuJoCo's humans and braking.** Under `BODY=mujoco` a human is a mocap body that touches the base by `Sim`'s rule, which holds the base while they touch, rather than pushing it as the ADR first proposed, because a pushing human of a world file's size and speed could squeeze the base out at meters a second. The owner chose on 2026-10-06 that the armor widens its keeps and the geofence by the distance the base still moves before it stands, which ended its closing on the geofence, solids and a standing human (PLAN, Known issue 33). What remains is a human who walks into the braking base: in ten mock missions it closed at most 2.5 mm a run, and the armor's command never points at the human. The session left that as it is; ending it would mean widening a human's keep by the human's own approach or braking harder.
- **The differential drive.** `DRIVE=differential` is an option beside the holonomic default (above). The base starts facing east, turns at up to 2 rad/s, drives only within 30 degrees of the command and never backward, and the armor checks the motion the base executes; `DRIVE` passes through `scripts/stage.sh`, so a demo or a scene can fly it ([safety](safety.md)).
- **The armor's holonomic restraint.** The session fixed the default body's armor although every earlier number was measured on it, since its promise of no motion toward a human inside 0.7 m and none into a solid failed for a few ticks after the acceleration limit (PLAN, Known issue 32). Missions and the demo's beats came out the same; squeezed between a pillar and a human, the body now stands where it slid before.
- **Worlds.** A world file holds at most 16 entities in 64 KiB, radii up to 5 m, walking speeds of 0.1 to 2 m/s and ids of up to 16 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens; `scripts/stage.sh` unsets `WORLD`, so each scene names its own; the default `MISSION` names the world's first beacon; humans may walk through obstacles, and a body can push a human who steps aside past the fence ([worlds](worlds.md)). `just test` also pins the default world in a release build, which takes the pre-commit hook to 10 to 11 s.
- **The scenes' stages.** Each canon scene plays a world of its episode, and Episode 18 is `ep18-bardiel`: Toji walks toward the body, the pilot leaves, the dummy plug's goto toward him fails 0/3 on the course rule, and a goto clear of him passes ([scenes](scenes.md)). In the Tokyo-3 stage of Episode 3 the body already stands at Shamshel when the power runs out, so the stop at zero shows on the panels, not the radar. The demo keeps the default world, so the hero GIF stands.

## Provisional, decided by the session of 2026-10-07

The owner had the session of 2026-10-07, which built Phase 3 Tasks 6 to 8, take the safe, recommended option wherever a decision was the owner's and list it here, as above.

- **The static musl recipe stays local.** `just musl-mujoco` builds the field unit with `-d mujoco` as a static aarch64 musl binary in an Alpine container, into `musl-mujoco/`, which the recipe owns and replaces whole after a build that succeeds; no CI job runs it and `release.yml` builds no `-d mujoco` binary, since every CI minute counts against the private repository and ADR-0008's Action Item 2 asks for a recipe only. It waits on `docker info` without a watchdog, since Ctrl-C ends it with one line; a daemon that hangs has not turned up. Revisit once a field computer flies `BODY=mujoco` (PLAN, Phase 3 Task 4).
- **License notices in the binaries' archives.** The recipe's output carries MuJoCo's notices and its dependencies', not gehirn's own LICENSE, since it is a local build nobody distributes. zenoh-c's license notice is in no archive yet, neither this output nor the release archives of `release.yml`, which predates this session; packing it means fetching the license at the pinned tag in `scripts/zenoh.sh`, and the owner decides before the first release is published.
