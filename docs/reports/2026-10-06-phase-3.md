## Measurements

Each number below comes from PLAN's State of 2026-10-06, where its raw data and caveats are named.

- **The default world stays as it was.** A release build sensed the new simulator beside the old one 14 million times without one difference, and a test pins it.
- **Bodies on the mock.** Holonomic `Sim`, differential `Sim` and the MuJoCo base each delivered 10 of 10, at a median of about 31.6 s, with the scenario gate held.
- **MuJoCo as a static aarch64 musl binary** delivered 10 of 10 in an Alpine container, which settles Invariant 9 for it.
- **The armor fix** took the holonomic body's lingering motion toward a human from 35 ticks to none, and fuzzing over 300,000 scenes found 43,159 violations before it and none after.
- **The course rule.** It changes nothing in the default world: no course fact in the mock missions, and S1 to S18 send the same requests byte for byte. With the veto in every unit, hosted lineup A held the gate on S1 to S22 at `magi-eval 10`. On `worlds/crossing.json` the fact held in 9 of 9 hosted cases and 27 of 33 on the mock, where the other 6 walkers arrived just past the 2 s horizon.
- **Hosted lineup A, ten missions per body**, each 10 of 10 with no contact:

| | Holonomic | Differential | MuJoCo |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery, median | 57.8 s | 85.8 s | 59.0 s |
| Nearest human at a delivering release | 2.49 m | 2.04 m | 2.00 m |

- **Scenes.** All five flew three times each with every beat in order and were recorded once; the recordings are linked from [docs/scenes.md](../scenes.md#recordings).
