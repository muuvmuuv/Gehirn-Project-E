## Measurements

Each number below comes from PLAN.md, from its State of 2026-10-07 or from the task or known issue the bullet names, where its raw data and caveats are named, and the teaser's from docs/launch.md.

- **The local planner against the reflex** (Task 6). On the mock, 26 sets of ten missions on `Sim` with both drives and on the MuJoCo base, over the default world, `worlds/example.json`, `worlds/crossing.json` and the scene worlds: none of the 260 missions had a contact, an armor refusal or a fault. The planner kept every human's rim at least 0.693 m from the body's center and every solid's at least 0.451 m, against the reflex's 0.268 and 0.350 m, and its MuJoCo base stepped toward a walker inside `human_stop` in no tick, against 646 under the reflex. Its cost on the default world is time: it reached b1 0.52 to 0.66 s later on a way 0.67 to 0.70 m longer and delivered one deliberation later on each body; on `worlds/example.json` it delivered 18.1 s sooner on `Sim` and 19.6 s on MuJoCo. On `ep13-iruel` and `ep18-bardiel` it never reached the beacon in 20 of 20 runs, where the reflex did in 20 of 20 (Known issue 38). Hosted lineup A flew 90 missions on `Sim`: the planner delivered 37 of 45 against the reflex's 33, the 4 more on `ep18-bardiel`, kept every human outside `human_stop` on the way, the closest at 0.721 m against 0.537 m, and drew 3 armor refusals against 8; at every release that delivered the human was at least 2.05 m away on either.

## Decisions

The owner had the session take the safe, recommended option wherever a decision was the owner's and list it in [docs/decisions.md](../decisions.md) under "Provisional, decided by the session of 2026-10-07", where each entry gives its reasons. They wait for review, as the section of 2026-10-06 still does:

- **The static musl recipe stays local.** No CI job runs `just musl-mujoco` and `release.yml` builds no `-d mujoco` binary; revisit once a field computer flies `BODY=mujoco`.
- **License notices.** The recipe's output carries MuJoCo's notices, not gehirn's LICENSE; zenoh-c's notice is in no archive yet, which the owner decides before the first release.
- **The world generator's model and rules.** google/gemini-3.8-flash at low effort by default; an approval is judged by MAGI's own limits and a rejected release as misjudged only 0.2 m past the 2.5 m line; every run gets only the configuration it names, so no key from `.env` reaches a mission.
- **S23 joins the gate**, and the mock's MELCHIOR-1 counts a target within `lcl.arrive` of a human's rim as that human's position; no persona was tuned.
- **The local planner.** `PLANNER=local` in a module of its own that imports only `lcl`, the reflex the default until the owner decides, `armor.Armor.top_speed` public, 0.15 m beyond `solid_keep` and a berth of 0.3 m beyond `human_stop` with a step toward a human inside the berth a cost, not a rule, and the seated circling of Known issue 38 left as it is.
- **`Sim`'s stopping distance.** 30 ms of travel a sense, which moves the default body by millimeters near a keep or the fence, so it no longer moves to the bit as it did.
- **The planner as the world generator's reference.** By default, with `PLANNER=` in `--ref-env` bringing the reflex back, and "no delivery" counting only runs that never came within reach of the beacon.
- **Terrain on the plane.** ADR-0010 stays Proposed until the owner accepts it; every new kind is a circle on the plane, and a world without one sends every request as before, byte for byte.
- **Ground.** One kind, a patch with a factor of 0.1 to 0.9 outside the scene, which lowers the armor's top speed; every model reads a line per patch, only in a world that holds one.
- **Ditches and cliffs.** One kind, `ditch`, kept at `solid_keep` as a pillar is, with no MAGI fact of its own.
- **Obstacles that move.** Kind `obstacle`, walking by a human's rules and reacting only with `stop`, at a keep of 0.5 to 3 m.
- **Falling objects.** A landing zone from the world's start that becomes a crater; a goto into it binds every unit to no, a landing time of 0 or less faults BALTHASAR-2, and a warning time waits for an ADR of its own.
- **A goto onto a human within reach** binds every unit to no, acting on the answers only, and S24 to S27 join the gate.
- **Episode 12.** `ep12-sahaquiel` joins the canon scenes; its GIF shows the release, and the refused catch is in the MP4.
- **HQ drops a malformed snapshot**, a context whose positions are not two numbers.
- **The interactive bridge.** A readout after 0.3 s, a pin on a click released within 300 ms, paging back by clicks alone; staging only with the mock's `--stage`, on the mock lineup with a simulated body, for loopback requests alone, and it cannot force BALTHASAR-2.
- **The teaser.** One real vote, then Episode 6's vote restaged and labeled as scripted, 28 s, silent, in 16:9 and 9:16, with the website's domain burned into the end card.

The owner's own decisions of this session are under Tooling there: V through proto, which keeps gehirn on V 0.5.2 until a release or snapshot builds it, and the interactive bridge, which reads and never decides.
