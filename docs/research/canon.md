## Project E

Project E (E計画, E-keikaku) is GEHIRN's program to build the Evangelion units, and after GEHIRN became NERV one of its three major projects beside the Human Instrumentality Project and the Adam Project. Ritsuko Akagi joined GEHIRN as its head, and her mother Naoko Akagi built the MAGI there. Sources: [Project E](https://evangelion.fandom.com/wiki/Project_E), [Ritsuko Akagi on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritsuko_Akagi).

Correction to a common reading: in Episode 2, "The Beast", Unit-01 does not run out of power; it shuts down after damage and the loss of the pilot's signal, then goes berserk. Episode 19 is the episode where the power runs out.
