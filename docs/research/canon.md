Researched on 2026-10-05 from the Evangelion Wiki on Fandom, EvaGeeks' EvaWiki, fan transcripts at animanga.com and evaotaku.com, and Wikipedia. ## Project E

Project E (E計画, E-keikaku) is GEHIRN's program to build the Evangelion units, and after GEHIRN became NERV one of its three major projects beside the Human Instrumentality Project and the Adam Project. Ritsuko Akagi joined GEHIRN as its head, and her mother Naoko Akagi built the MAGI there. Sources: [Project E](https://evangelion.fandom.com/wiki/Project_E), [Ritsuko Akagi on Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ritsuko_Akagi).

| 7 | End of Evangelion, Episode 25' (Unit-02) | The JSSDF cut Unit-02's cable, Asuka counts about 20 s of battery per Mass Production Eva, the battery runs dry and the dummy-flown Evas tear it apart. | The cut cable and the hold at zero |

| 9 | Episode 20, "Weaving a Story 2: oral stage" | Shinji's sync passed 400% and his body dissolved into LCL; a salvage a month later fails before he returns. | The top of the sync ratio; the core's share stays under its ceiling |

Weaker fits: Episode 22, "Don't Be" (Asuka's sync drops until she is made backup, benching); Episode 5, "Rei I" (Unit-00 runs out of control in a test and its plug ejects, the restraints); Episode 7, "A Human Work" (Jet Alone has a reactor, no power limit and a remote link that fails, a foil for the umbilical); Episode 11, "The Day Tokyo-3 Stood Still" (a blackout, with emergency power to the MAGI).

Correction to a common reading: in Episode 2, "The Beast", Unit-01 does not run out of power; it shuts down after damage and the loss of the pilot's signal, then goes berserk. Episode 19 is the episode where the power runs out.
