# Scenes

Scenes from the series, flown on the mock. Each is a script in `scripts/scenes` that stages part of the world, such as a unit the mock forces to vote one way or a cable cut at a set moment, and leaves the rest to the mock's script and gehirn's code. The mock scripts the core and MAGI (`tools/mock_endpoint.py`), so a scene shows how the stack reacts, not how real models judge. At 0:00 it says in the terminal what is staged and what is real, then narrates each beat with the time since the start, as `just demo` does. The canon behind each is in [research/canon.md](research/canon.md).

## Flying a scene

```sh
just scene=ep13-iruel demo
just scene=ep13-iruel demo-record
```

`scene` names a script in `scripts/scenes`; `demo`, the default, is the whole mission of [Running gehirn](running.md#the-demo), and the parameters are the demo's. Scenes are staged on the mock, so they fly on `lineup=mock` only, and `just` refuses a scene on another lineup, or a name that is not a script there, before anything builds. A beat that does not come in time stops the scene with one line that names the log it waited on. The demo plays the default world of [Worlds](worlds.md), on whose walking human its beats are timed, so the stage unsets `WORLD` with the other variables. Each scene then sets `WORLD` to a world of its episode in `worlds/`, on which its own beats are timed, and says at the field unit's start who walks on it. Each section below says what in the episode every element of its world stands for. The clocks in the tables are those of three flights on 2026-10-06, and `ep12-sahaquiel`'s on 2026-10-07 (PLAN, State), a range where they differ.

`demo-record` writes `gehirn-<scene>.mp4`, from MAGI deliberating on the first goto, or from the field unit's start in a scene without one, to just after the last beat, with a 40 s grace at 8x where the scene kills HQ. Where the scene shows a vote on an irreversible proposal it also writes `gehirn-magi.gif`, the MAGI block from the first such vote to the last.

In canon, an Eva without power or pilot can go berserk, and the commanders overrule the MAGI. gehirn takes the other side on purpose ([decisions.md](decisions.md#stack), the stance): losing control ends in the safe state, and nothing turns MAGI's no into a yes. Where a scene departs from canon, it says so.

## ep13-iruel: Episode 13's MAGI hack

- **Canon:** in Episode 13, "Lilliputian Hitcher", the Angel Iruel takes over MELCHIOR, which calls for NERV HQ's self destruct. The other two refuse, so it takes BALTHASAR, and CASPER holds until Ritsuko's counterprogram lands with about a second left. She works it from inside CASPER, through a hatch into its casing, with Maya beside her and Misato waiting on them, while the operators call out each unit that falls, and Gendo has the Evas shot to the surface without their pilots, away from the Angel ([Evangelion Wiki](https://evangelion.fandom.com/wiki/Episode:13), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilliputian_Hitcher), [transcript](https://www.animanga.com/scripts/textesgb/eva13.html)).
- **World:** `worlds/ep13-iruel.json`, NERV HQ's MAGI room. The obstacles melchior, balthasar and casper are the three MAGI, towers in the triangle the bridge's MAGI panel draws. The beacon hatch is the opening Ritsuko works through, against CASPER's south face. Ritsuko walks from the consoles to the hatch and stands on it, and Maya and Misato come to stand on either side of her. Hyuga and Aoba, the operators who call out the hack, stand at their consoles. The body is Unit-01, pilotless and alone at the east end of the floor.
- **Staged:** the mock's core proposes `self_destruct` on every request, a verb outside the schema it is asked for (`tools/mock_endpoint.py --propose`). MELCHIOR-1 approves it from the first vote, BALTHASAR-2 from the second, and CASPER-3 refuses until the third (`--vote`). BALTHASAR-2 runs on a chat model, as lineup C does, because on Jev gehirn rejects every verb outside `lcl.known_verbs`.
- **Real:** an unknown verb counts as irreversible (Invariant 2), so it needs all three units, and one holdout blocks it (Invariant 3). BALTHASAR-2's first no is unforced, the mock's scripted answer to a verb it does not know. HQ's cooldown spaces the votes. Even 3 of 3 does not move the body: the armor permits only the verbs in `Limits.verbs` and refuses `self_destruct` (Invariant 5). The body never gets a goal and stays at its start. The room moves no beat: the units refuse the unknown verb before they read a distance, the armor refuses it before it measures one, and no human comes nearer the body than Misato, at 3.9 m, so none stops or steps aside for it.
- **Differs from canon:** in canon the three units share one personality, so the Angel can take them one by one. gehirn runs three model families (Invariant 4), which one exploit would not take alike, so the mock forces each fall. In canon MELCHIOR calls for the self destruct, but in gehirn MAGI only judge and only the core proposes, so the Angel holds the core too: a hacked core and three fallen units, and the armor still refuses. And in canon Ritsuko stops the hack in time; here CASPER-3 falls too, to show that MAGI's approval is never enough. The body stands in the MAGI's room, since the bridge draws only what the body senses, and the hatch is a beacon the core never sends it to. A walk starts with the world, so Ritsuko, Maya and Misato set off for CASPER before MELCHIOR-1 falls; in canon they go after it.
