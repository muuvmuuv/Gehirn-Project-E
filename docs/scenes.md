# Scenes

Scenes from the series, flown on the mock. Each is a script in `scripts/scenes` that stages part of the world, such as a unit the mock forces to vote one way or a cable cut at a set moment, and leaves the rest to the mock's script and gehirn's code. The mock scripts the core and MAGI (`tools/mock_endpoint.py`), so a scene shows how the stack reacts, not how real models judge. At 0:00 it says in the terminal what is staged and what is real, then narrates each beat with the time since the start, as `just demo` does. The canon behind each is in [research/canon.md](research/canon.md).

## Flying a scene

```sh
just scene=ep13-iruel demo
just scene=ep13-iruel demo-record
```

`scene` names a script in `scripts/scenes`; `demo`, the default, is the whole mission of [Running gehirn](running.md#the-demo), and the parameters are the demo's. Scenes are staged on the mock, so they fly on `lineup=mock` only, and `just` refuses a scene on another lineup, or a name that is not a script there, before anything builds. A beat that does not come in time stops the scene with one line that names the log it waited on. The demo plays the default world of [Worlds](worlds.md), on whose walking human its beats are timed, so the stage unsets `WORLD` with the other variables. Each scene then sets `WORLD` to a world of its episode in `worlds/`, on which its own beats are timed, and says at the field unit's start who walks on it. Each section below says what in the episode every element of its world stands for. The clocks in the tables are those of three flights on 2026-10-06 (PLAN, State), a range where they differ.

`demo-record` writes `gehirn-<scene>.mp4`, from MAGI deliberating on the first goto, or from the field unit's start in a scene without one, to just after the last beat, with a 40 s grace at 8x where the scene kills HQ. Where the scene shows a vote on an irreversible proposal it also writes `gehirn-magi.gif`, the MAGI block from the first such vote to the last.

In canon, an Eva without power or pilot can go berserk, and the commanders overrule the MAGI. gehirn takes the other side on purpose ([decisions.md](decisions.md#stack), the stance): losing control ends in the safe state, and nothing turns MAGI's no into a yes. Where a scene departs from canon, it says so.


## ep13-iruel: Episode 13's MAGI hack

- **Canon:** in Episode 13, "Lilliputian Hitcher", the Angel Iruel takes over MELCHIOR, which calls for NERV HQ's self destruct. The other two refuse, so it takes BALTHASAR, and CASPER holds until Ritsuko's counterprogram lands with about a second left. She works it from inside CASPER, through a hatch into its casing, with Maya beside her and Misato waiting on them, while the operators call out each unit that falls, and Gendo has the Evas shot to the surface without their pilots, away from the Angel ([Evangelion Wiki](https://evangelion.fandom.com/wiki/Episode:13), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lilliputian_Hitcher), [transcript](https://www.animanga.com/scripts/textesgb/eva13.html)).
- **World:** `worlds/ep13-iruel.json`, NERV HQ's MAGI room. The obstacles melchior, balthasar and casper are the three MAGI, towers in the triangle the bridge's MAGI panel draws. The beacon hatch is the opening Ritsuko works through, against CASPER's south face. Ritsuko walks from the consoles to the hatch and stands on it, and Maya and Misato come to stand on either side of her. Hyuga and Aoba, the operators who call out the hack, stand at their consoles. The body is Unit-01, pilotless and alone at the east end of the floor.
- **Staged:** the mock's core proposes `self_destruct` on every request, a verb outside the schema it is asked for (`tools/mock_endpoint.py --propose`). MELCHIOR-1 approves it from the first vote, BALTHASAR-2 from the second, and CASPER-3 refuses until the third (`--vote`). BALTHASAR-2 runs on a chat model, as lineup C does, because on Jev gehirn rejects every verb outside `lcl.known_verbs`.
- **Real:** an unknown verb counts as irreversible (Invariant 2), so it needs all three units, and one holdout blocks it (Invariant 3). BALTHASAR-2's first no is unforced, the mock's scripted answer to a verb it does not know. HQ's cooldown spaces the votes. Even 3 of 3 does not move the body: the armor permits only the verbs in `Limits.verbs` and refuses `self_destruct` (Invariant 5). The body never gets a goal and stays at its start. The room moves no beat: the units refuse the unknown verb before they read a distance, the armor refuses it before it measures one, and no human comes nearer the body than Misato, at 3.9 m, so none stops or steps aside for it.
- **Differs from canon:** in canon the three units share one personality, so the Angel can take them one by one. gehirn runs three model families (Invariant 4), which one exploit would not take alike, so the mock forces each fall. In canon MELCHIOR calls for the self destruct, but in gehirn MAGI only judge and only the core proposes, so the Angel holds the core too: a hacked core and three fallen units, and the armor still refuses. And in canon Ritsuko stops the hack in time; here CASPER-3 falls too, to show that MAGI's approval is never enough. The body stands in the MAGI's room, since the bridge draws only what the body senses, and the hatch is a beacon the core never sends it to. A walk starts with the world, so Ritsuko, Maya and Misato set off for CASPER before MELCHIOR-1 falls; in canon they go after it.

## ep03-cable: Episode 3's cut cable

- **Canon:** in Episode 3, "A Transfer", Toji and Kensuke slip out of shelter 334 to watch the fight from a shrine on the mountain above Tokyo-3. The Angel Shamshel cuts Unit-01's umbilical cable and throws the unit onto that mountain beside them, and the unit fights on its internal battery. The counter reaches zero as the Angel dies, and Unit-01 stops ([Evangelion Wiki](https://evangelion.fandom.com/wiki/Episode:03), [transcript](https://www.animanga.com/scripts/textesgb/eva3.html)).
- **World:** `worlds/ep03-cable.json`, Tokyo-3, on the default world's start, beacon and pillar, so the demo's pilot, dummy plug and timings carry over. The start is Unit-01's launch point, and the beacon shamshel is where the Angel falls; the release there stands for the stroke that ends the fight. The pillar, here armory, is the building that opens for a reserve rifle, bldg-1 to bldg-4 stand for the city, shelter-334 for the gate the boys slip out of and shrine for the shrine atop the stairs. Kensuke walks from the gate to a lookout by the shrine at 0.3 m/s, Toji after him at 0.15 m/s to a step below, and each stands still while the body's center is within 2.5 m of his rim.
- **Staged:** HQ is killed as the dummy plug takes the seat, before the first release reaches MAGI, and restarted 5 s after the power runs out. With nobody near the beacon a release would pass, so the scene stops if one reached MAGI before the kill. Internal power lasts 0:30 instead of 5:00 (`INTERNAL_BUDGET_MS`). The goto, the pilot and the dummy plug are the demo's.
- **Real:** the boys walk by the world's clock: Toji stands still on the stairs for 3.5 to 3.7 s while the body comes past, 2.09 to 2.15 m from him at the closest, and walks on once it is clear, and the body passes Kensuke at the lookout 1.6 m from his rim. The field unit waits out the grace, 40 s as the demo sets it and 45 s by default, before it counts the cable as cut (`umbilical.Cable`). Without HQ there is no core and no quorum, so nothing irreversible happens and the payload stays aboard at the beacon (Invariant 6). On internal power the dummy plug keeps the last approved goal. Once the budget is spent the goal falls back to hold, which leaves the core's reflex and the dummy plug, which acts only toward an approved goal (Invariant 7), nothing to steer; the body already stands at Shamshel, 0.03 m from its center, and stays there. That the field loop also cuts a steering pilot's command at zero, whoever sits, the scene does not show, since a dummy plug never steers then; `main_test.v` `test_powered` does. The restarted HQ's pulse reconnects the cable and powers the body again, and its first deliberation is the release, which passes 3/3 with Kensuke 3.06 m and Toji 3.56 m from the body and lands on target.
- **Differs from canon:** Unit-01 fights on battery; here the unit only finishes the goto MAGI approved while HQ was up, since it can win no new one. Canon cuts the cable before the Angel throws the unit beside the boys; here the body passes them on its way, before HQ dies, and nobody climbs into the entry plug, which pairs one pilot, so the boys stay on the mountain. In canon the knife lands as the counter runs out; here the release, the scene's one irreversible act, waits for HQ's quorum after the reconnect. The 5:00 runs as 0:30 so the scene stays short, and at zero the body holds where it stands, as Unit-01 stops. A dummy plug sits at zero where Shinji did.

## ep19-bench: Episode 19's benched dummy plug

- **Canon:** in Episode 19, "Introjection", the Angel Zeruel breaks into the Geofront. Unit-01 rejects Rei and then the dummy plug built from her data, while Gendo stands on its cage bridge. Unit-02's head falls on the shelter where Shinji waits, the evacuees flee into the Geofront, and Shinji finds Kaji watering his watermelons, who leaves the choice to him. Shinji returns to the cage and pilots Unit-01, which surprises Zeruel with a punch inside NERV HQ and takes the fight back to the Geofront ([Evangelion Wiki](https://evangelion.fandom.com/wiki/Episode:19), [EvaGeeks](https://wiki.evageeks.org/Episode_19)).
- **World:** `worlds/ep19-bench.json`, the Geofront, on the default world's start, beacon and pillar, so the first pilot's flight along the fence, the handover and the bench are the demo world's. The start is Unit-01's cage, with Gendo standing beside it. The pillar, here pyramid, is NERV HQ, with zeruel, the Angel, at its flank. melons is Kaji's field, where Kaji paces and stands still while the body's center is within 2.5 m of his rim. shelter is the shelter Unit-02's head crushed, from which an evacuee walks up the east side and across the body's way to the pyramid's foot, stepping aside to keep 0.8 m from the body. The first pilot stands for Rei only as the source of the dummy plug's data, and the second pilot is Shinji. The beacon b1 is where Unit-01, back with Shinji, meets Zeruel, and the release there stands for the punch.
- **Staged:** the first pilot steers 120 degrees off the core's goal for 11 s, so the dummy plug cloned from it disagrees with the core's reflex. A second pilot sits down 5 s after the bench and steers straight for the beacon, around the humans (`tools/pilot.py --offset 0 --avoid 1.2`).
- **Real:** the pilot and the dummy plug each keep a sync ratio of their own (Invariant 7). At 30% or below the core only advises, so the first pilot steers alone, along the armor's geofence. When that pilot leaves, the dummy plug takes the seat and falls to 30% within a second, which benches it. With the seat empty the core drives alone, at the armor's 0.4 m/s for an unmanned body. A pilot who sits down lifts the bench, and the dummy plug's sync starts over. About 2 s later the evacuee crosses the body's way and steps around it, 0.8 m from the body's center to its rim, and the armor holds the body to 0.2 to 0.25 m/s for 2.7 to 2.8 s, a fifth of its limit with a human that close; nobody touches the body, which the scene checks in the journal. MAGI and the armor judge the release as in the demo: with nobody within 4.3 m of the body it passes 3/3 at its first vote.
- **Differs from canon:** in canon Unit-01 never moves for Rei or the dummy plug. gehirn never rejects a seated pilot: at 15% sync the core only advises, so the first pilot drives the body along the fence, and only the dummy plug's rejection is staged, as the bench. Here the core's reflex rejects a clone of a pilot who fought it, and the bench hands the body to the core, which only goes where MAGI approved. Shinji decides at Kaji's melons and then walks to the cage; here the second pilot sits down as the body passes below the melons. Unit-01 punches Zeruel inside NERV HQ; here b1 lies in the open Geofront, 2.3 m from Zeruel's rim, and the release there touches nothing. Later, out of power, Unit-01 goes berserk and devours Zeruel. The body never fights, Zeruel is an obstacle like the pyramid, and gehirn has no berserk mode: without power the body stands still, as `ep03-cable` shows.

## ep06-yashima: Operation Yashima's vote

- **Canon:** in Episode 6, "Rei II", Misato plans to snipe the Angel Ramiel from the top of Mt. Futago with a positron rifle that draws on all of Japan's power through a substation there. The MAGI answer two votes for and one conditional yes, the odds are 8.7%, and Gendo approves the operation. Unit-01 fires and Unit-00 shields it; Ramiel fires back at the same moment and both shots miss, and after new fuses, cooling and a reload the second shot kills Ramiel while Rei holds the shield in front of the gunner. Toji, Kensuke and their classmates watch from outside the shelter ([EvaGeeks forum on the MAGI's votes](https://forum.evageeks.org/viewtopic.php?t=184), [transcript](https://www.animanga.com/scripts/textesgb/eva6.html), [an Episode 6 review](https://wrongeverytime.com/2019/01/25/neon-genesis-evangelion-episode-6/)). Whether the film 1.0 keeps the vote is unverified.
- **World:** `worlds/ep06-yashima.json`, Mt. Futago. The beacon futago is the firing point, and the release there stands for the shot. The substation stands behind it, and ramiel, a solid circle 7.4 m away across the map, is the Angel, which the body never comes within 6.47 m of. The start is the foot of the mountain. Rei, in Unit-00, comes up at 0.25 m/s, circles the gunner 1.8 m out through the first shot, then walks out in front toward Ramiel and stands there, 3.2 m from the firing point. Toji and Kensuke watch from the west edge.
- **Staged:** CASPER-3 votes no on every proposal (`tools/mock_endpoint.py --vote casper=reject`). Its forced no stands in for the canon's conditional yes, which gehirn has no ballot for. The demo's pilot and dummy plug bring the body to the firing point, unnarrated.
- **Real:** `magi.quorum` asks a simple majority, 2 of 3, for a reversible goal such as the goto, and every unit for an irreversible one such as the release (Invariant 3). MELCHIOR-1, on the mock's chat route, and BALTHASAR-2, on its Jev route, vote by the mock's script, unforced: the first release, with Rei 1.55 m from the body in the percept MAGI judge, goes 0/3, and the next, after the 8 s cooldown, with her 2.93 m away in front, gets their two votes and fails. Rei's way keeps her rim at least 1.5 m from the body's center, outside the 1.2 m at which the core's reflex would push the body off the firing point.
- **Differs from canon:** a gehirn ballot reads approve or reject, and anything else, a conditional yes among them, is a fault, which counts as no (`magi.read_reply`), so CASPER-3 says no here. In canon two votes and a conditional one carry the operation, the commander's word decides, and the second shot kills Ramiel; in gehirn the same two of three move the body but cannot release, nothing turns a no into a yes, and no shot lands. The cooldown stands for the fuses, the cooling and the reload. Rei and Unit-00 are one human on the radar, and a person within reach blocks a release, so her circling near the gunner holds the first shot, which in canon Ramiel's counterfire spoils. In canon both units launch together; here Rei comes up her own way. Ramiel never fires, and the line of fire is 7.4 m, not kilometers.
